Chasing the midnight sun across five countries — over 20 hours of daylight a day, from Reykjavík's volcanic coastline to Stockholm's island-studded archipelago. Join us on this exciting trip.

The raw, dramatic landscapes of Iceland and Scandinavia completely redefine what it means to experience the great outdoors. This region is a world of extreme contrasts, where Iceland's active volcanoes and steaming geothermal pools clash with massive glaciers and glowing ice caves. Across the water, Scandinavia unfolds into the sheer, majestic cliffs of Norway's deep fjords, the endless pine forests of Sweden, and Finland's labyrinth of pristine lakes.
